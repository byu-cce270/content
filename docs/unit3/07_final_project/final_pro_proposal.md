## Instructions

1. Read the [final project requirements](final_pro_prompt.md) and the [final project reading](final_pro_read.md) as a team. Then find a problem worth solving, using the guidance in [Choosing a Problem](final_pro_prompt.md#choosing-a-problem), and get a TA's or the instructor's approval for it.

2. Following Steps 1–3 of the design process in the reading, write `spec.md` in your team repository. It must include:
      - Purpose, Users, Inputs, Calculations, Outputs, Screens, and Out of scope
      - Platform and structure: web or desktop, and the files you plan to have
      - Design review: the agent's suggestions and what your team decided about each one
      - Validation plan: at least three hand-worked cases with exact inputs and expected outputs (with sources), plus property checks and edge cases
      - Team workflow: how you will split the work (one driver, everyone coding, or a mix) and who is responsible for what

3. Make a Gantt chart in Excel that schedules the project from now until the presentations. Your Gantt chart should include:
      - The project name and team members
      - The tasks that need to be completed, including writing the spec, working the validation cases, building each feature, testing, writing the documentation, and preparing the presentation
      - The person responsible for each task
      - The start date and duration of each task, and the order in which they must be done
      - Milestones, such as "validation cases pass" and "all features working"

    For a refresher, see the [Gantt Chart Reading](../../unit1/5_gantt_charts/gantt_read.md){:target="_blank"} from Unit 1.
